Spendly Prompt Log

## Entry 1: Plan the app

Technique used: Giving the AI information about the app and what it should do

Purpose: Plan an app that helps people keep track of their money.

Prompt:
> I’m making an app called Spendly. It helps students and other people keep track of money they earn and spend. Plan an app with sections for a home page, adding transactions, receipts, statistics, and bill reminders. Users should be able to set budgets and save their information in the browser.

What the AI suggested: It suggested the main parts and features for the app.

What I checked: The app needed to work without an account or server. It also needed to save information in the browser.

What I changed or improved:
> Keep the app simple and make sure users can move between the sections. Save their information in the browser.

## Entry 2: Add transactions and receipts

Technique used: Listing what a feature needs to do

Purpose: Let users add and find income and expenses.

Prompt:
> Add a form where users can record income or expenses. Include the amount, category, date, description, and an optional receipt picture. Show a message if required information is missing or incorrect. Let users search for transactions and save them in the browser.

What the AI suggested: It suggested a transaction form, messages for mistakes, receipt pictures, search, and browser saving.

What I checked: The form should explain mistakes clearly. Saved transactions should still be there when the app is opened again.

What I changed or improved:
> Let users edit or delete a transaction. Keep old transactions when a new one is added.

## Entry 3: Add statistics, budgets, and reminders

Technique used: Improving the app by adding details

Purpose: Help users understand their spending and remember bills.

Prompt:
> Add a Statistics section that shows monthly income, expenses, and spending by category. Let users set or remove a budget for a category. Add a Reminders section where users can add, edit, and delete bill reminders. Save budgets and reminders in the browser.

What the AI suggested: It suggested monthly summaries, category budgets, and bill reminders.

What I checked: Users should get a clear message when something is saved or removed. The app should explain what to do when a section is empty.

What I changed or improved:
> Show a helpful message when there are no reminders. Mark reminders that are due today or are overdue.

## Entry 4: Make the app easier to use

Technique used: Checking the result and asking for improvements

Purpose: Make Spendly easier to use on phones and computers.

Prompt:
> Make Spendly work on phones, tablets, and computers. Keep the Home, Add Transaction, Receipts, Statistics, and Reminders sections easy to find. Make the forms easy to read and show which button or field is selected when using a keyboard.

What the AI suggested: It suggested ways to organize the app for different screen sizes and make the controls easier to use.

What I checked: I needed to try the app on different screen sizes and check that the buttons and forms worked.

What I changed or improved: I tested the sections, forms, search, budgets, reminders, and saved information. I wrote down any problems I found and how I fixed them.

## What I learned

AI helped me plan and improve Spendly. I checked its suggestions and made changes so the app could track income and expenses, show receipts and statistics, manage budgets and reminders, and save information in the browser.
